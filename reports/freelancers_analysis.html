Now let's take a look at the Pearson's correlation coffectient to see the correlation in a

And also we should take a look the the P value to see how signifcant is "Hours worked"

correlation and sigficance between 'Hours worked' and 'Hour rate'

Pearson's r = 0.19805630889942963
P value     = 0.0018790952327925575
